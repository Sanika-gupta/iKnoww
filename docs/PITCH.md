# iKnoww — the 100-word pitch

<!-- pitch:start -->

iKnoww is a watchlist for stocks and mutual funds that knows why you're watching. Every item carries one thesis — buy below ₹X, or protect at ₹Y — and a regression against the market splits each price move into market noise and real, stock-specific signal. That single number decides what the card says, whether it alerts you, and how much friction sits before your trade fires. A stop-loss triggering on a market-wide fall gets flagged, not fired blindly. Corrections retract their own alerts. There's no alert-configuration screen — your thesis is the rule. Questions get answered from live data, never invented.
